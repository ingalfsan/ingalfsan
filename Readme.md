## 🔭 Active Projects & Current Focus

### 🤖 RAG Architectures & Private Backend Systems

I'm currently building **private RAG-in-a-Box** systems — self-contained Retrieval-Augmented Generation pipelines that allow companies to upload documents, auto-index them, and query them in natural language via authenticated REST APIs. These systems are powered by **Gemini**, **FAISS** vector stores, and **FastAPI**, running on private infrastructure for maximum data security.

I'm also developing **Natural-DB**, a system that converts natural language questions into SQL queries over PostgreSQL databases using LLMs, deployable as a private microservice.

### 💬 Conversational AI for Insurance & Electronic Banking

I build **AI-powered chatbots** integrated with **WhatsApp** and **Telegram** for the insurance and banking sectors. These bots quote insurance policies, handle customer interactions, and provide intelligent, real-time responses using APIs from **OpenAI** and **Gemini**. My work in this space bridges the gap between legacy backend systems and modern conversational interfaces.

### 📊 ML-Driven Financial Systems

**Coin Prophet** — A crypto market prediction platform using TensorFlow neural networks and Firebase for scalable, real-time ML inference. I'm also co-authoring research on **fuzzy logic architectures for market reversal classification** and **adaptive trading systems** based on RSI pattern accumulation.

## 🎓 Professor & Maker — Open Hardware Educator

I'm a **university professor** at the Instituto Universitario de Tecnología de los Llanos (IUTLL), where I teach courses on **Algorithms & Programming**, **Programming Paradigms**, and **Software Engineering**.

### What I do in the classroom:

- **Open hardware in education**: I've researched and published on using **hardware libre** (open-source hardware like Arduino and Pinguino boards) to teach programming languages. My work of academic ascent at IUTLL formally studied this approach.
- **Maker of educational electronics**: I build custom boards (like the **Pinguino board** — a low-cost, license-free Arduino alternative) as hands-on teaching tools for my students to experiment with microcontrollers.
- **Pedagogical materials on advanced topics**: I design teaching resources on:
  - **Hexagonal Architecture** (Ports & Adapters) for clean, maintainable backend design
  - **Database Security** — best practices for securing PostgreSQL, encryption at rest, row-level security, and role-based access control
  - **Algorithms & Data Structures in C** — complete study guides with solved exercises and practical labs
